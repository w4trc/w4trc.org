### N4I On the Air from the Netherland Inn

_October 17, 2026_

Every year, the Kingsport Amateur Radio Club sets up special event station **N4I** at the historic Netherland Inn. This year was no exception: we spent the day on the air, met people, and made contacts across the country and beyond.

**A little history**

The site was built in 1802 as a boat yard for shipping salt. It was later sold, and in 1818 it became the Netherland Inn, a stop for travelers on the way from Middle Tennessee to Western Kentucky. More than two hundred years later, it's still a great place to tell its story over the radio.

**The setup**

We ran two stations this year:

| Band | Radio | Antenna |
|---|---|---|
| 40M | Yaesu FT-991A | Wolf River Coil vertical |
| 20M | Icom IC-7300 | End-fed half-wave tossed into a nearby tree |

**How the day went**

Over six hours on the air, we logged **28 contacts**. Operators took turns at the radios throughout the day, and plenty of folks stopped by just to visit.
