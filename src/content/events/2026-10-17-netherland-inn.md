Come join us for our annual Netherland Inn Special Event Station. We will be celebrating the anniversary of Netherland Inn by running a special event station on HF. Come, hang out, and run a radio to make contacts all over the world.

We will also be doing a walking fox hunt during the event, bring your own fox hunting equipment or use equipment we will have and try to find the foxes within walking distance of Netherland Inn.
